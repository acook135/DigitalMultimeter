Start here — how to take a measurement

  1. Turn the dial to the function you need (click a function name, or drag the knob).
  2. Check the red lead is in the right jack: VΩ for volts, ohms and continuity — 10 A for current. Black always stays in COM.
  3. Place the probes: drag each tip onto a labelled test point. The point rings in the probe colour when contact is made.
  4. Read the display, then press Check my measurement.
Volts → in parallel, across two points, circuit live.
Amps → in series: open a joint and bridge the gap with the probes.
Ohms → circuit dead and isolated, component disconnected.

Welcome to the Digital Multimeter Workshop

This trainer lets you practise using a digital multimeter (DMM) on a 12 V headlamp circuit – safely. You can measure volts, amps, ohms and continuity, find faults with volt-drop tests, and even blow a fuse without any cost.

  • Learn the meter: the dial, the jacks and the probes
  • Take real readings from a live model of the circuit
  • Work through 10 guided tasks with a live set-up checklist
  • Diagnose a hidden fault like a real technician

Would you like a guided tutorial? It takes about 5 minutes and highlights every part of the circuit, the meter and each button.

You can start the tutorial again at any time with the ? Tutorial button at the top right.
